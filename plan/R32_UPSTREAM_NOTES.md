Original opening identity and unload/getter ordering remain covered by [R11's upstream note](R11_UPSTREAM_NOTES.md): app identity tokens and early public unload subscriptions do not provide a new Core atomic startup subscription. A future public durable per-opening identity/status receipt should replace only the corresponding compensating adapter after the same close/reopen and original-context controls pass.

The R30 exact receive-type capability deferral remains recorded in [the API decisions](API_DEFERRALS_20261115.md). R32 adds no probing address generation or private capability classifier.

CSV staging, version consent, original-snapshot capture and cleanup are app responsibilities over existing file-policy ports. The rejected/retired CSV frame repair fixes app ownership; it is not attributed to a missing Wallet/Loader capability or proposed as a Core/Qt patch. Native Qt chooser dependency issues remain recorded and deferred; no custom chooser is introduced.

Actual focused counts/source identities live in the R32 acceptance receipt. Exhaustive coverage, full configuration/platform/compatibility matrices and sanitizers remain R42 work under the user's cadence.
